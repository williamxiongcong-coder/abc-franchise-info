Confidentiality Agreement

PARTIES. This agreement is between the person named in the acceptance below, together with their heirs, successors and related entities (the "Recipient", "you"), and ABC Franchise Pty Ltd, acting for itself and on behalf of the franchisor of the Uncle Joe's Malaysian Kitchen brand and its related entities (the "Discloser", "we", "us").

BACKGROUND. The Discloser has developed valuable intellectual property and confidential information relating to the business identified by the "Uncle Joe's Malaysian Kitchen" brand. You have expressed interest in a Uncle Joe's Malaysian Kitchen franchise and have requested access to Confidential Information in order to advance discussions. You covenant to protect that information on the terms below so that the Discloser is able to freely disclose it.

1. Definitions. "Approved Purpose" means the evaluation of the Uncle Joe's Malaysian Kitchen franchise opportunity, including obtaining legal, accounting and business advice. "Confidential Information" means all information relating to the Discloser, its related entities or the franchise disclosed to you, and specifically includes: (a) any financial data or business information, including unit-level financial performance; (b) the contents of any operations or procedures manual or any document describing the business format, systems, techniques, recipes, procedures, policies or practices; (c) any document or information relating to trade marks, names, logos, internet sites, products, colour schemes or other intellectual property; (d) any copyright material, including manuals, brochures, recipes, menus and schedules; (e) any price lists, supplier terms, customer lists or customer information; (f) territory plans and the franchise Disclosure Document; and (g) any information marked "private and confidential" or provided on a confidential basis.

2. Confidentiality obligations. You must: (a) keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential and not disclose it in any manner without our prior written consent; (b) use it only for the Approved Purpose and no other purpose; (c) not copy, reproduce or make written notes of it without our prior written consent, and deliver up any permitted copies or notes immediately on request; (d) provide secure and proper storage and take all precautions necessary to prevent accidental disclosure; (e) keep a record of any copies made and by whom, and supply that record on request; and (f) ensure these obligations are observed by any employee, officer, agent, contractor or adviser engaged by you, for whose breach you remain strictly liable. These obligations are in addition to those imposed by the general law.

3. Permitted disclosure. You may disclose Confidential Information only to your professional advisers (lawyer, accountant, financier) and those of your employees, officers and agents who need it solely for the Approved Purpose, each of whom must be bound by obligations of confidence no less strict than this agreement, or as required by law. We may object to disclosure to any particular person, in which case you must not disclose to that person.

4. Public domain. These obligations do not apply to information that is in the public domain through no fault of yours — but the burden of showing that any information is in the public domain rests solely with you.

5. Return or destruction. If you do not proceed, or if we request at any time, you must immediately return or destroy (as we direct) all Confidential Information, including all notes, documents, copies and electronically stored material, and if required certify in writing that you have fully complied.

6. No reliance. Except as otherwise agreed in writing, we make no representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information, accept no liability for any loss arising from any inaccuracy or incompleteness in it, and take no responsibility for any interpretation, opinion or conclusion you form from examining it.

7. No offer; no obligation to proceed. Nothing in this agreement obliges either party to proceed with any transaction. Providing information to you is not an offer of a franchise. Either party may end discussions by written notice at any time. Any grant of a franchise will follow the disclosure and cooling-off process required by the Franchising Code of Conduct.

8. Non-compete covenant. You must not use the Confidential Information, or assist or permit any related person or entity to use it, to establish, acquire or participate in any business or opportunity similar to or competitive with the Uncle Joe's Malaysian Kitchen franchise, or otherwise to the competitive disadvantage of the Discloser. You must not challenge the Discloser's ownership of the Confidential Information or the brand's intellectual property.

9. Remedies. You acknowledge that the Confidential Information is at all times the property of the Discloser, that a breach of this agreement would be harmful to the Discloser's business interests, and that monetary damages alone would not be a sufficient remedy. In addition to any other remedy available in law or equity, the Discloser is entitled to interim, interlocutory and permanent injunctions to prevent breach and to compel specific performance.

10. Indemnity. You indemnify the Discloser, its related entities and their officers, employees and advisers against all claims, losses, costs and expenses (including legal costs on a solicitor-and-client basis) arising directly or indirectly from any breach of this agreement by you or by any person to whom you have provided Confidential Information.

11. Survival. Your obligations under this agreement survive the finalisation or discontinuance of discussions, continue for three (3) years from the date of your acceptance below, and bind and benefit the parties' successors and assigns.

12. Confidentiality of discussions. You must not publicise the existence or contents of this agreement, the exchange of Confidential Information, or any matter related to the discussions, without our prior written consent.

13. Entire agreement; severance; variation. This agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings. If any provision is void or unenforceable it is severed, and the remainder continues in full force. Any variation must be in writing and agreed by both parties. Each party bears its own costs of entering into this agreement.

14. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia, and the parties submit to the jurisdiction of its courts.

15. Electronic acceptance. You agree that completing and submitting this form, including typing your full legal name below, constitutes your acceptance of this agreement, may be relied on by the Discloser as such, and has the same effect as a handwritten signature. A formal Confidentiality Deed in execution form (signed and witnessed) may additionally be required before certain materials are released.
